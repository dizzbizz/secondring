Questions

The ones I get asked.

Does this replace my voicemail?

No. Your voicemail works exactly as it does today. This runs alongside it — the difference is direction. Voicemail waits for him to do something. A text reaches out while he’s still holding his phone.

What if my wife answers the phone?

Then it never fires during the day. It only kicks in when nobody picks up at all, so it covers the evenings, the weekends and the times she’s out. If every call gets answered, it costs you nothing and sends nothing.

Will it text people I was about to call back?

Only if you don’t pick up. We set the ring window a couple of rings longer than your normal answer time, and I adjust it in the first week once we see how your calls actually come in.

Do I have to change my number?

Never. Your number stays exactly where it is, on your truck, your website and your Google listing. If you cancel, there’s nothing to move back.

What happens when the customer texts back?

It comes to your phone as an ordinary text message. No app to open, no portal to log into. You reply from your own messages like you would to anyone else.

Is this one of those AI things?

No. It’s a phone line and a text message you wrote yourself. Nothing pretends to be a person and nothing answers on your behalf.

What does it cost to get started?

Nothing for the first two weeks. After that it’s $69.99 a month, no setup fee, month to month. If it isn’t earning its keep I’ll take it off rather than bill you for it.
